The App acts on your instructions against storage accounts that you
configure. You are responsible for:

- the credentials you enter and the permissions they grant;
- the objects you upload, change, move or delete. Deleting, renaming,
  moving and mirror sync with deletion can remove data at your provider
  immediately; the App's trash keeps a local copy only within the
  limits you set;
- the charges your storage provider bills you for storage, requests and
  data transfer. The bandwidth limit in the App limits throughput only,
  not provider charges;
- your encryption keys. If you use client-side encryption and lose or
  delete the key, the encrypted objects cannot be decrypted. The
  Publisher has no copy of your keys;
- presigned links you share. Anyone with such a link can access the
  object until the link expires;
- keeping your own backups.
