# Support and update record

How the DigitalFreedom support and update policy applies to Bucketeer.
The binding text for users is the EULA, section 9
(`Bucketeer/Resources/Legal/EULA.md`).

transition note of the policy (offered plus two years) does not apply.

## Commitment

At least five years of security and conformity updates for each version
from the day that version is placed on the market. The end month and
year appear in the Mac App Store listing and in the About window.

Security and conformity updates are distinct from new features, from
support for macOS versions Apple has dropped, and from continued sale
of the App or Bucketeer Pro.

## Adaptation to Bucketeer

- The App runs locally on the Mac. The Publisher operates no server and
  no user account, so the email notice to account holders in policy
  section 4 has no counterpart. A withdrawal is announced in the App
  and on https://getbucketeer.app at least 30 days in advance.
- Bucketeer Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It does
  not renew, so the subscription wind-down and pro-rata refund rules of
  policy section 4 do not apply.
- Withdrawal removes the store listing only. Installed copies and
  existing Bucketeer Pro purchases keep working, and security and
  conformity updates continue until the stated end of the support
  period.
